How the forecast is built

  • Start: latest Net Worth checking balance, rolled forward by transactions since.
  • Income: auto-detected cadence from your paycheck history.
  • Bills: from recurring bills — set a Day-of-month for each.
  • Goals: monthly contributions from goals (toggleable above).
  • Everything else is approximated as a constant daily burn rate from last 3 months.

Tip: Conservative mode (p75) picks your busier months as the baseline. Use it to stress-test.
